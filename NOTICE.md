The workflow design was informed by these public projects; this repository implements its own source records, review ledgers, model task validation, and publication workflow rather than redistributing those repositories or their sample books:

- [PDF-to-EPUB](https://github.com/yuhanwang14/PDF-to-EPUB): per-page checkpoints, per-book configuration, chapter/footnote/image assembly and Pandoc publication.
- [ChineseBookOCR](https://github.com/huutrungle2001/ChineseBookOCR): scanned Chinese book processing, review-oriented workflow design and OCR comparison ideas.
- [pycorrector](https://github.com/shibing624/pycorrector): long-text splitting and source-positioned correction outputs. Its models and correction implementation are not bundled.

Runtime components remain subject to their respective upstream terms:

- [PyMuPDF](https://github.com/pymupdf/PyMuPDF) / MuPDF: PDF rendering and EPUB inspection; consult upstream licensing for the chosen distribution.
- [OpenCC](https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC): traditional/simplified conversion.
- [Pillow](https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow): image operations.
- [certifi](https://github.com/certifi/python-certifi): CA bundle for verified HTTPS fallback.
- [PaddleOCR](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR), PaddleX and PaddlePaddle: optional OCR backend; model weights are downloaded separately by the user.
- [CnOCR](https://github.com/breezedeus/CnOCR) and RapidOCR: optional second-engine comparison.
- [Pandoc](https://github.com/jgm/pandoc): separately installed EPUB compiler, invoked as an executable rather than redistributed here.

The offline demo's short text and geometric illustration are original fixtures generated by this project. No commercial book excerpts, scanned pages, converted books, provider account configuration, or real model responses are included in the public repository.
